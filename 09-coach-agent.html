09 Answers from the notes

AI Sifu Yik: your coach agent

Everything in this training is also indexed inside an agent. Ask it a question and you get an answer that comes from these notes — so you don't have to rewatch a video to find one line.

A coach agent is not a smarter chatbot. It is this training, searchable. The value is the same value as notes: you get the answer that was already in the room.

09.1What subscribers get

  • Access to all of my AI training, notes and resources.
  • AI Sifu Yik: an AI agent with access to all of my notes and training. Ask a question and get an answer that comes from my training, so you do not have to watch the video to find it.
  • An overview of the AI Avatar training and the Facebook Mastery training, with the option to upgrade to the full versions.
Where to open it

The agent lives inside the subscriber hub. Open it from the same place you access the training notes — the link is in your welcome email. If you can read the notes, you can reach the agent.

09.2Three questions to try live

  1. “Which image model should I use for a minimal infographic?”

    A direct lookup. Watch how fast it answers from the notes rather than from the open web.

  2. “How do I set up a scheduled price-tracking task?”

    A multi-step setup. This is where an agent trained on notes beats a general chatbot.

  3. “Give me a 7-day plan to prepare for Black Friday for my [niche].”

    A planning task. It should reuse the timeline and the prompts from section 08.

09.3How to use the coach well

  • Ask a specific question with your situation: “I sell [X] to [Y]. What should I do first?”
  • Ask for a checklist or steps, not only an opinion.
  • Ask “which lesson or note is this from?” so you can go deeper.
Scope

The agent is a coach based on the training. It is not legal, tax or financial advice.

09.4Your next step

Pick one section of this training and apply it to one real thing this week — one post, one offer, one prompt you actually run. That is the difference between having notes and having a business.
