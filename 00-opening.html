Everyone asks which model is best. The better question is which model fits the job you are already paid to do. This section is the frame for everything that follows.

00.1The argument in one line

Newer models give you faster and better output, but they do not guarantee better quality or better results. What makes money is the business model: a clear niche, a repeatable content system, an audience you own, and an offer.

00.2What you get today

  • The best, alternative and free option for every tool type — text, image, video, agents, publishing.
  • Live demos of how I actually use them, including the parts that fail.
  • A plan for Black Friday and Q4, when most sales happen.
The rule for the whole training

Do not pay for a tool unless you really need it. Do not invest in subscriptions until you are already making money from what you have.

00.3The three-question test

Before you use or buy any tool, answer these three questions. If you cannot answer the third, close the tab.

  1. What exact job does this tool do in my business?

    Not what it can do in a demo. What job does it replace, speed up or make possible this week?

  2. Is there a free option that does 80% of this job?

    Most of the picks in this training are free on their own platform. Pay only when the free limit stops you.

  3. Will this tool help me make money in the next 30 days?

    If the answer is no, it waits. A useful tool with no job is still a subscription.

00.4What changed since the core training

  • Models are faster and better, especially for video (longer clips with sound) and images (cleaner text and infographics).
  • Agents now do multi-step work for you instead of only answering in chat.
  • Cheap APIs let you run big volume at small cost.
  • The skill that matters is picking the right tool for the job and giving it the right reference.
Takeaway

It is not about the model. It is about the model for your business.

00.5Use the same niche all session

Every demo today runs through one niche (I use the same one all the way through). Pick yours now — a page, a product, or a skill you want to sell — and keep answering every prompt with it. That is how the demos become your plan instead of my screenshots.
