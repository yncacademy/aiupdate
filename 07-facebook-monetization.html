07.6Bonus idea: the Ghibli cooking page

Food is visual, easy to understand with no language, and a recipe gives people a reason to save and share. It also runs on one master look, which means you are not redesigning every post.

  1. Pick one angle (example: cozy Ghibli-style home cooking, one dish per post).

  2. Create a master look: a consistent kitchen scene and style prompt. Save it.

  3. For each dish, ask Claude or DeepSeek for a short recipe (ingredients + 5 steps) in simple English.

  4. Generate the dish images, or a 10–15 second clip (Seedance 2.0 for under 15 seconds).

  5. Post as photo + recipe text, a carousel with steps, and one short Reel.

  6. Schedule a week at a time.

  7. Reverse engineer the winners and make more.

Prompt Recipe + visual 
Create a recipe post for [DISH]. Give me: a hook line (max 12 words), ingredients list,
5 short steps, and one tip. Then write an image prompt for a warm, hand-painted
Ghibli-inspired kitchen scene showing the finished dish. Same style every time:
soft light, painted textures, cozy colors, no text in the image.
Reminder

Be original, follow the AI-label rules, and do not use copyrighted characters or logos.
