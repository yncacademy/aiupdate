Section 01 · Text models

Prompt adaptation

Template copy and adapt 
Act as [ROLE]. My business is [BUSINESS]. My audience is [AUDIENCE].
Task: [TASK].
Format: [FORMAT, e.g. table, 5 bullets, script].
Rules: simple English, short sentences, no filler.
Before you answer, ask me up to 3 questions if anything is unclear.
Section 01 · Text models

Starter research

Prompt copy and adapt 
Research [TOPIC] for [AUDIENCE]. Give me: (1) what is new in the last 30 days,
(2) what people are asking or complaining about, (3) 5 content angles,
(4) 3 sources I should read. Mark anything you are not sure about.
Section 03 · Image models

Reverse engineer an image

Prompt copy and adapt 
Analyze this image. Describe: layout, text hierarchy, color palette, fonts style,
lighting, camera angle, and mood. Then write one prompt I can use in an image model
to recreate this style with my own topic: [YOUR TOPIC]. Do not copy any logos or text.

Section 07 · Facebook monetization

Recipe + visual

Prompt copy and adapt 
Create a recipe post for [DISH]. Give me: a hook line (max 12 words), ingredients list,
5 short steps, and one tip. Then write an image prompt for a warm, hand-painted
Ghibli-inspired kitchen scene showing the finished dish. Same style every time:
soft light, painted textures, cozy colors, no text in the image.

Content calendar

Prompt copy and adapt 
Create a 14-day content calendar from [DATE] to Black Friday and Cyber Monday for [NICHE].
Each day: format (photo+text, carousel, Reel, email), hook, key point, call to action.
Mix 70% value, 20% proof, 10% offer. Simple English.

Email sequence

Subject lines under 45 characters. Each email under 150 words.
