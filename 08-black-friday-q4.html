08 The part that pays for the year

Use AI to conquer Black Friday and Q4

People are ready to buy, and more of them will ask AI what to buy. Your offer has to be clear enough for both a person and a model to understand. Here is the timeline, the prompts, and the buy list.

Q4 is not a content sprint. It is a short window where people who already want to buy need a reason to buy from you. AI is how you make enough versions of that reason in time.

08.3Seven steps, adapted for solo creators

  1. Pick one goal.

    Total sales, new email subscribers, or first-time buyers. One number.

  2. Pick a margin-safe offer.

    Do not discount so deep you lose. Add a bonus instead of cutting the price.

  3. Map your calendar.

    Teaser, launch, mid-sale reminder, last call.

  4. Get your page ready.

    Clear offer page, simple checkout, fast answers to the top 10 questions.

  5. Reach past customers and subscribers first.

    They buy fastest.

  6. Promote on two or three channels.

    Where your audience already is — not where you wish they were.

  7. Follow up after the sale.

    Thank-you, support, ask for feedback.

08.4AI prompts for Q4

Prompt Offer builder 
I sell [PRODUCT/SERVICE] to [AUDIENCE]. Normal price: [PRICE]. Goal: [GOAL].
Give me 5 Black Friday offers that protect my margin. For each: the offer, the bonus,
the deadline, and one risk. Recommend one.
Prompt Content calendar 
Create a 14-day content calendar from [DATE] to Black Friday and Cyber Monday for [NICHE].
Each day: format (photo+text, carousel, Reel, email), hook, key point, call to action.
Mix 70% value, 20% proof, 10% offer. Simple English.
Prompt Email sequence 
Write 5 emails: teaser, launch day, midday reminder, last call, thank-you.
Product: [PRODUCT]. Offer: [OFFER]. Tone: friendly, direct, no hype.
Subject lines under 45 characters. Each email under 150 words.
Prompt FAQ + support agent 
Based on this offer page [PASTE], write answers to the 15 questions buyers will ask.
Keep each answer under 40 words. Flag anything the page does not answer.
Prompt Post-sale 
Write a thank-you message and a 3-question feedback form for people who bought [PRODUCT].
Then write 3 post ideas that show real customer results, without exaggerating.
The last prompt is a warning

“Without exaggerating” is the important part. Fake results are the fastest way to lose an audience you spent a year building.

08.5Q4 content ideas by niche

Facebook-friendly formats that are already working in Q4.

  • Gift guides, “deal checklists”, and “what to buy vs skip” posts.
  • Holiday recipes and cozy content (works with the Ghibli cooking idea).
  • Budget and money-saving tips.
  • “Year in review” and “next year plan” posts.
  • Fake discount claims.
  • Fake countdowns.
  • Copied product images.

08.7Rules for buying on Black Friday

  1. Only buy a tool for a job that is already making money or about to.

  2. Check the normal price first. A deal is only a deal if the base price was real.

  3. Prefer a monthly plan you can cancel, unless you have used the tool for three months or more.

  4. Be careful with lifetime deals on AI tools. AI costs money to run — check what happens to usage limits, and whether the company will still exist later.

  5. Put a cancel reminder in your calendar the day you subscribe.

  6. Use one card with a limit for trials.

  7. Do not buy “just in case”. Write down the task first.
