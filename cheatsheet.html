Reference CExtra tools to consider

  • Voice and audio: a voice tool for voiceovers (for example ElevenLabs) and Adobe Podcast Enhance for free audio clean-up, mentioned in the Avatar notes.
  • Research notebook: a notebook-style tool that answers only from documents you upload — useful to show the idea behind AI Sifu Yik.
  • Design: Canva for quick layouts and social templates.
  • Automation: a no-code automation tool to move content from a sheet to a scheduler.
