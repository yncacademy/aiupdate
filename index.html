The rule for todayDo not pay for a tool you do not need

The three-question test from the opening, before you use or buy anything.

  1. What exact job does this tool do in my business?

    Not “what can it do” — what job does it replace or speed up.

  2. Is there a free option that does 80% of this job?

    Most of the picks in this training are free on their own platform.

  3. Will this tool help me make money in the next 30 days?

    If the answer is no, it waits.
