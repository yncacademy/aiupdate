01 Pick the right brain

Text models: best, alternative, free

Get the right AI for the job, then stop paying for the ones you do not use. Most of these are free on their own platform — pay only when the free limit stops you.

One model is not better than another in the abstract. Claude writes better, DeepSeek is cheaper at volume, Grok sees X, Perplexity lets you switch brains mid-conversation. Pick by job.

01.2What I pay for, and why

DeepSeek API / DeepSeek Chat

A small top-up lasts a very long time. DeepSeek Flash is the best cost-to-value I have found — it handles writing, coding and agent work. Even $10 goes a long way. For chat only, the website is completely free.

Grok through X Plus

Only $8 a month, and it also gives you the verified badge and enough daily use of Grok, images and video: Grok Chat, Voice, Imagine and Build, Grok API access, Cursor, plus the X Premium checkmark.

ZenMux (unified API)

Like OpenRouter, but with pop-up promotions — for example extra bonus credit when you recharge. It also lets me reach models that are hard to access from my location, compares models in one chat, and has a generous free model.

01.3Live demo A: ZenMux

One account. Many models. Real cost. This is the demo that stops people from buying five subscriptions.

  1. Open ZenMux, create an account, add a small credit amount.

  2. Show the model list: pick one Claude, one GPT, one DeepSeek, and a free model.

  3. Use the built-in Chat and send the same prompt to two models.

  4. Show the usage page so you see the cost of one task.

  5. Create an API key. Explain: this key is like a password — never share it or post it in a screenshot. Then show how to connect it to another app.

Why a unified API

One account and one balance for many models. You can test a new model the day it launches without a new subscription.

01.4Live demo B: why you need a prompt list

A prompt list is not a list of winning prompts. It is a brainstorm tool. Most of the time we do not know what to ask AI to do — reading the list shows you jobs you never thought of.

  1. Open your prompt collection.

  2. Scroll through three categories quickly (writing, image, video).

  3. Pick one prompt you have never used and run it live on your own business.

  4. Show how to adapt a prompt: change the role, the audience and the output format.

01.5How I research

  • Perplexity for deeper research when I write and plan. (Update: use Projects instead of Spaces.)
  • Grok for the latest news and discussions, because it can see X.
Prompt Starter research 
Research [TOPIC] for [AUDIENCE]. Give me: (1) what is new in the last 30 days,
(2) what people are asking or complaining about, (3) 5 content angles,
(4) 3 sources I should read. Mark anything you are not sure about.
